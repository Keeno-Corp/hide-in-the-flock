# Hide in the Flock (storybook pastoral)
## What it is
Browser three.js game (plain page, no build step; index.html, game.js, fx.js, geo.js, fonts/): a sheep hidden among ~100 AI sheep in a dusk meadow; a wolf has 3 bites. Solo (AI wolf hunts the player) and local 2-player on one keyboard (second player is the wolf). The page is improved in place, not rebuilt. The look is held to LOOK.md, CHARACTERS.md, refs/sheets/LOOK.png and the references in refs/ (01 Fink pastoral painting, 03 Vogel woodcut) and refs/study/ (layered-hill and flat-meadow frames). STYLE IS JUDGED AS HARD AS GAMEPLAY.
## Must have
1. Looks as LOOK.md says, and every one of its Checks holds: a storybook illustration, not a 3D game. Flat painted masses with wobbly dark-brown ink outlines, a paper/gouache grain over the whole frame, a hand-painted dusk sky with cloud bands and a low warm sun, long raking indigo shadows, layered haze-faded hills, and cream sheep with dark faces. The wolf is a stark near-black silhouette and the darkest shape on screen. No faceted generic low-poly, no neon colours, no glossy surfaces.
2. `compare` against refs/01.jpg and refs/sheets/LOOK.png keeps improving or holding; the judge names the biggest remaining style gap each round.
3. The clip moment is the whole point. The wolf's pounce (slow-mo leap, camera punch-in, sting) leads to the bite beat: an ink splash, a held woodcut duotone freeze frame, and a tilted stamped card slamming in. A real player gets "HUMAN" in red ink with GOTCHA and PLAYER 1; an AI sheep gets "SHEEP" with "baa?" and the bite is lost. It happens within the first minute of solo play and reads clearly in a 5-second clip.
4. Plays as DESIGN.md's Core loop and Controls say. Solo is genuinely fun and fair: the wolf's suspicion is readable, blending in works (grazing, drifting with the flock), the 3 clover patches are findable and drawn in the storybook style, and the win and lose screens are stamped storybook cards with restart.
5. Local 2-player on one keyboard is playable and fun for the wolf player: pounce targeting is clear and in-style, with 3 bites.
6. UI is storybook typography (IM Fell English / SC, Alfa Slab One stamps) on cream paper panels with ink borders. It is readable in a laptop browser window of any size, and the narrator caption is a storybook line.
7. Keeps the window.__game convention, starts playing on load, plain page with no build step, ~100 instanced sheep, and a light post pass (60fps target on a desktop GPU).
8. Juice in the mood: hushed dusk sound (wind, soft baas, munch, a gong and stab on the bite, silence before the stamp thunk), sheep animation (grazing head bob, hops) and wolf animation.
