# Hide in the Flock
## What it is
Browser three.js game: a sheep hidden among ~100 AI sheep; a wolf has 3 bites. Solo (AI wolf) and local 2-player.
## Must have
1. Looks as LOOK.md says: its Checks hold
2. Plays as DESIGN.md's Core loop and Controls say
3. Pounce clip moment: ink splash, held duotone frame, stamped card (HUMAN / GOTCHA / PLAYER 1, or SHEEP / baa?), within first minute solo
4. ~100 instanced sheep at 60fps; window.__game convention
