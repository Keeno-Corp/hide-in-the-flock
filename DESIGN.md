# Design doc: Hide in the Flock
## Core loop
Blend into ~100 AI sheep, graze at clover (hold E) three times, survive 3:00 while a wolf builds suspicion on odd movement and pounces.
## Controls
WASD/arrows walk, Shift run, E/Space graze, M toggles Solo/Local. Local: P1 WASD/Shift/E; P2 arrows move wolf, Enter pounce.
## Progression
Single 3-minute round, restart with R.
## Win and lose
Solo: 3 clovers + 3:00 wins; eaten loses. Local: sheep wins at 3:00 or wolf out of 3 bites.
## Levels
One dusk meadow ringed by hills, pines and stone walls (no island or sea).
## Feel
Slow-mo pounce, camera punch-in, sting, then the bite beat: ink splash, held duotone frame, stamped card slams in (HUMAN red stamp + GOTCHA + PLAYER 1, or SHEEP + baa?).
## Audio
fx.js synth baas, munching, wind, sting.
## UI
Cream paper panels with ink borders, IM Fell / Alfa Slab type, bites as ink paw/wolf pips, storybook narrator caption.
## Decisions
Tone: comic nature documentary (builder, unasked). Wolf AI wastes bites on AI sheep early in solo.
